# Owned reference camera corpus — CC0-1.0

All fixture data and the silent PCM WAV are original MapLab project work, dedicated to CC0-1.0. The source closure copies our owned placeholder fixtures. No third-party art, music, engine code, or recordings are used. The WAV contains sixteen zero-valued mono 16-bit samples at 8 kHz; it is an asset-reference witness, never played. `index.json` hashes every final fixture byte other than itself.

## Qualified identity and observations

`maplab.reference-camera-preview@1`, `canCertifyEngine:false`, is a read-only presentation observer, separate from the source `maplab.camera@1` contract and from physics. This corpus certifies only the published reference preview rules. It claims no engine camera, mixer, audio import/export, effect-handler, landing-prediction, or gameplay movement-modifier parity. Audio references and effect calls are displayed as intent, never fetched or executed.

`cases.json` supplies owned source maps, camera/environment overrides, rectangular actor collider and exact 1/256-WU physics-state observations. First observations and explicit `reset` entries initialize the observer without smoothing. Short/tall jumps, ladder and long drop use resolved reference-controller ticks; the ladder schedule derives from our owned Test-runtime ladder fixture. The shaft translates owned solid bodies, footholds and traversal/access geometry downward by 1408 WU. Stacked floors are deliberately camera-only support/air observations, not a certified traversable route or a physics witness. Other cases exercise presentation inputs directly. Input ticks are consecutive except at explicit reset. Expected files record every preview sample; browser and Node must agree exactly.

## Numerical rules

At each resolved 60 Hz physics tick, strict full-body interior overlap at the current pose selects camera/environment zones; tangency is outside. Highest numeric priority wins, then ascending ASCII stable ID. Environment selection is independent per kind. Equal highest-priority active camera/audio/visual overlaps emit a warning. Modifier and launch intent has no contract priority, sorts at zero and is configured only.

Effective size is viewWU / zoom. A free-axis target uses actor center, horizontal velocity times twelve ticks capped by absolute lookaheadWU.x, and downward velocity times twelve capped by absolute lookaheadWU.y. Dead zones use normalized left/top/width/height. A reset aligns the target to the dead-zone center. Thereafter a free axis moves only enough to keep the target in its dead zone. Platform snapping holds Y during short jumps; crossing the dead-zone upper edge with the actor head follows the safety margin. Falling more than one-quarter view height below last grounded foot enables capped downward follow. A higher landing after air/climb snaps to the new support once. ClimbFollow uses continuous vertical following while climbing. Continuous mode always uses the dead zone. A disabled follow axis, or locked vertical policy, centers that axis on the effective bounds.

Free-axis damping advances by (desired-current)/max(1,dampingTicks). A zone change blends numeric settings from the previous effective settings over the incoming transitionTicks using linear t or t²(3−2t); the first entered sample is 1/N and the Nth is complete. Incoming discrete flags apply immediately. Free centers blend from the previous displayed center; locked axes remain exactly at the interpolated bounds center. Interrupted blends start from the last effective settings. Free target centers round half away from zero to 1/256 WU before confinement. Visible rectangles, rather than actor targets, are confined. An axis with smaller bounds centers and reports symmetric letterboxWU. Thus the final bounds clamp may produce sub-1/256 coordinates when zoom/bounds require them.

Audio reports the selected track, loop, fadeTicks, previous zone and elapsed/progress intent. Entry uses incoming fade, exit uses outgoing fade, first sample is 1/N, interruption starts a fresh intent transition, and reset selects immediately. These are intent counters, not audio amplitudes or playback. Ambient, effect and shake references remain data. Pause, event injection, overlay toggles and UI publication do not sample. Test reset/replay and portal arrival rebuild smoothing using the destination state.

Preview inputs are owned/frozen. Schema, contract identity, normalized dead-zone sums, bounds range, region references, declared audio MIME and actor precision/tick sequencing are checked before state commits. Limits: 256 zones of each family, polygon ≤256 vertices, aggregate sensor-work estimate ≤100000 (vertices³ per polygon, 16 otherwise), ≤3601 samples between resets. Unavailable preview reports a diagnosis separately; physics continues unchanged.

## Independent proof

`tests/consumer/camera.mjs` uses Node standard libraries and only these final fixture bytes. It checks hashes, rect-zone membership/priority, identity, zoom/bounds/letterbox/dead-zone algebra, locked centers at every transition sample, analytic lookahead and damping/reset values, support snapping, and fade progress. It separately reconstructs boss numeric transitions. Its oracle is qualified to this owned rectangular-zone corpus, not arbitrary polygon geometry or an engine implementation. Tests rehash coherent numerical corruption and wrong zone winners to prove semantic rejection beyond checksums. Runtime tests additionally prove physics invariance and reset/portal integration.
