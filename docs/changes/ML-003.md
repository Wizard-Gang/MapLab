2. **Adoption of architecture revision R1.** Commit the owner-approved revision

   rejects duplicate keys before values can be discarded, the ID and catalog-key

   header subset (header, metadata, required features, coordinates, bounds,
   collision contract, dependencies, assets). Publish the local
   `schemas/maplab.map.v1.schema.json` for that subset and golden valid and invalid
   fixtures under `fixtures/`.

## Reason

ML-003 is the first consumer of the format rules R1 changes, so adopting R1 here
costs nothing to migrate. The owner approved option A on 17 October 2026: adopt R1
at ML-003 rather than inside ML-002 or not at all. The base parser and schema are
the source-scope deliverable of the adopted J2 row.

## Impact

Documentation: the adopted specification now states R1's quantum, range, tick
clock, controller, storage, layer and format vocabulary; the original 16 October
text stays in history at the ML-001 commit. Code: a pure `src/contracts/` module
with no React, DOM, editor, storage or network imports, plus a local schema file
and owned fixtures. The application shell, dependencies and lockfile are unchanged.
ML-009's reserved scope changes from IndexedDB-first storage to the library folder.

## Risk

A spec revision could hide scope drift. Mitigations: R1 is copied byte-for-byte,
edits are limited to the delta-table sections and the ML-009 row, and the queue
checker still enforces 44 sequential IDs with verbatim J2 text. A hand-written
parser can accept inputs a standard parser would reject, or the reverse; golden
and differential tests compare it with `JSON.parse` on every accepted input.
Header-subset validation does not yet cover geometry, gameplay or movement fields;
those remain ML-004–006 and are reported as unvalidated, not accepted.
